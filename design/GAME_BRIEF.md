# Pig Hunter — One-page game brief

## High concept

An endless top-down 3D action runner where a mounted hunter rides through stylized countryside, holds off increasingly dangerous pig herds, and builds a hunting party of loyal dogs and unusual weapons.

## Camera and presentation

- Orthographic 3D camera, high and diagonally offset.
- The hunter and horse remain near the lower-middle of the play area.
- Terrain streams toward the camera to create speed while the endless route is recycled.
- Strong silhouettes, warm earth tones, dusty movement, bright projectiles, floating damage, and clear pickups.
- Reference screenshots guide composition, readability, and pacing—not characters or copyrighted assets.

## Core loop

1. Steer the horse to avoid threats and line up targets.
2. The equipped weapon aims and fires at pigs in range.
3. Companion dogs independently intercept or damage nearby pigs.
4. Defeated pigs yield score, coins, and occasional upgrades.
5. Survive increasingly dense waves and periodic elite encounters.

## Initial content

- Hunter, horse, and one dog.
- Rifle plus future shotgun and repeating crossbow.
- Runner, sprinter, and brute pigs.
- Grassland trail environment.
- Endless score run with wave escalation.

## Design pillars

- **Readable chaos:** many threats, but targets and impacts remain easy to read.
- **Mounted momentum:** everything should sell forward speed and the horse's weight.
- **Helpful companions:** dogs visibly create breathing room and feel valuable.
- **Build variety:** weapons, dogs, and upgrades should produce meaningfully different runs.
- **Playful danger:** energetic and dramatic without gore or realistic animal harm.

## Prototype success test

Within the first minute, movement must feel responsive, shooting impacts must be satisfying, the dog must be noticeably useful, and rising pig pressure must force steering decisions.
